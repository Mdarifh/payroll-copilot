# Full and final settlement policy (Demo Tech Pvt Ltd)

Demo company policy. Leave encashment and notice recovery formulas are company
settings (FNF_* configuration), not statutory rules.

## Components

- Final month salary is prorated up to the last working day (LWD) with normal
  statutory deductions.
- Leave encashment = (Basic / 30) × leave days to be encashed. It is taxable but
  not part of PF wage or PT wage in the demo configuration.
- Notice pay recovery = (monthly gross / 30) × notice shortfall days, where the
  shortfall is notice period days minus notice days served.
- Other recoveries are deducted as recorded on the settlement.

## Tax

- The balance income tax for the financial year is recovered in the final
  month, because no further salary months remain.

## Gratuity

- Gratuity is not calculated by the system until a gratuity rule is configured.
  The settlement shows the length of service so eligibility can be checked.
