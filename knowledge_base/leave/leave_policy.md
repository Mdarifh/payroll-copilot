# Leave policy (Demo Tech Pvt Ltd)

Demo company policy describing how this payroll system is configured. It is not a
statement of law; check your state's Shops and Establishments Act for minimums.

## Leave types and yearly entitlement

- Casual leave (CL): 12 days per financial year (April to March).
- Sick leave (SL): 12 days per financial year.
- Earned / privilege leave (EL): 18 days per financial year.
- Leave without pay (LOP): unpaid; reduces paid days and salary.
- Balance = entitlement − approved leave − leave waiting for approval.

## Applying and approval

- Leave is applied by the employee and approved by the reporting manager or HR.
- Only approved leave is paid. A pending leave request does not protect a day from LOP
  until it is approved.
- An absence without an approved leave is shown to HR as an LOP candidate in the
  monthly payroll review.

## Half days

- A half day counts as 0.5 day present and 0.5 day absent.
- The absent half is an LOP candidate unless covered by an approved half-day leave.

## LOP decision

- HR decides the LOP days for the month in the payroll LOP review (Payroll Runs) or by
  editing LOP on the employee's attendance (with a preview and approval).
- LOP is placed on the absence dates (earliest first) so that PF can be split by date
  when a statutory rule changes inside the month.
