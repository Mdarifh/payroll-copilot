# Salary arrears policy (Demo Tech Pvt Ltd)

Demo company policy describing how the engine handles retrospective revisions.

## As-paid principle

- When a revision is effective from an earlier month but paid later, the earlier
  month is treated as paid at the old amounts. The difference is paid as arrear
  lines in the payment month.
- Arrear for a month = (new amount − old amount) × paid days / basis days of that
  month, for prorated heads.

## Statutory impact

- PF on arrears is calculated month by month using the PF rule and wage ceiling
  of the month the arrear relates to: PF on the revised salary minus PF on the
  old salary of that month.
- ESIC, PT and income tax treat arrears as wages of the month they are paid.
  Arrears do not change ESIC coverage.

## Uploads

- Uploaded arrear amounts are compared with the expected amount per head; a
  difference is reported as a mismatch with both values.
